📝 Research Notes
GCSE vs A-level national rankings

A-level ranks differ markedly. Waldegrave: top 5% GCSE → top 20% A-level (rank 486th); Grey Court: top 10% → top 25% (554th); Orleans Park: top 10% → top 25% (603rd). Three reasons: A-level ranking uses % A*/A not A*–B; sixth form cohorts are broader as external students join at 16; only 50–60% of Waldegrave Year 11 girls stay on — top performers often leave for Tiffin Girls or Esher College.

University destinations — Russell Group vs top-third

Russell Group (24 universities) is a self-selected lobbying group, not a quality benchmark. DfE is dropping it from official accountability measures. Better alternatives: Sutton Trust 30 (includes Bath, St Andrews, Loughborough, Surrey, Lancaster, Royal Holloway — all non-RG); DfE "top third" (high tariff, entry UCAS points). Grey Court (Good Schools Guide): ~50% RG but ~70% "top third." Orleans Park 2025: 61% RG but Bath specifically named (not RG). Waldegrave: 48% RG (third-party only). Key non-RG destinations include UAL (48.4% offer rate — 10th most selective UK university), conservatoires, RADA, Guildhall, Bath, St Andrews, Surrey.

Where Waldegrave Year 11 girls go for A-levels

Only 50–60% stay at Waldegrave sixth form. Rest go to:

  • Tiffin Girls — top academic draw; selective for sixth form entry
  • Esher Sixth Form College — Outstanding, 2,100 students, 61% A*–B, strong arts culture
  • RuTC — 5-min walk from Waldegrave; 24 A-levels + vocational; ~33% A*–B
  • Independent sixth forms — Hampton, LEH etc.
  • BRIT School, ballet/specialist schools

Waldegrave recruits ~30% of sixth form places externally, partly offsetting cream lost to Tiffin/Esher.

Kingston Academy — why A-level looks modest

56% A*–B is above the 47% national average. Standout metric is Progress 8 — top 0.5% nationally (32nd/6,500) — exceptional value-added for a non-selective comprehensive in a mixed-deprivation area. Sixth form still young and building.
