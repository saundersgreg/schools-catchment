📝 Research Notes
GCSE vs A-level national rankings

A-level ranks differ markedly from GCSE. Waldegrave drops from top 5% (GCSE) to top 20% (A-level, rank 486th); Grey Court top 10% → top 25% (554th); Orleans Park top 10% → top 25% (603rd). Three reasons: A-level ranking uses % A*/A not A*–B; sixth form cohorts are broader as external students join at 16; and for Waldegrave, only 50–60% of Year 11 girls stay on — top performers often leave for Tiffin Girls or Esher College.

University destinations — Russell Group vs top-third

Russell Group (24 universities) is a self-selected lobbying group, not a quality benchmark. The DfE is dropping it from official accountability measures. More meaningful alternatives: Sutton Trust 30 (30 most selective universities, includes Bath, St Andrews, Loughborough, Surrey, Lancaster, Royal Holloway — all non-RG); DfE "top third" (high tariff, based on entry UCAS points).

Grey Court (Good Schools Guide): ~50% Russell Group but ~70% "top third" — a 20-point gap showing significant non-RG selective destinations (Exeter, Nottingham, Surrey etc.). Orleans Park 2025: 61% RG but Bath specifically named as a destination (not RG). Waldegrave: 48% RG (third-party only — school doesn't publish this). Given the arts/media catchment, key non-RG destinations include UAL (10th most selective UK university by offer rate, 48.4%), conservatoires, RADA, Guildhall, Bath, St Andrews, and University of Surrey (very local).

Where Waldegrave Year 11 girls go for A-levels

Only 50–60% stay at Waldegrave's own sixth form. The rest go to:

  • Tiffin Girls' School — top academic draw for highest achievers (mainly 8s/9s at GCSE). Selective for sixth form entry.
  • Esher Sixth Form College (Thames Ditton, ~20 min) — Outstanding Ofsted, 2,100 students, open access from grade 4+, 61% A*–B. Major draw for arts/creative students.
  • RuTC — Richmond upon Thames College — 5-min walk from Waldegrave. 24 A-levels + vocational routes inc. Architecture, Fine Art, Music Technology. Results ~33% A*–B. Explicitly names Waldegrave among feeder schools.
  • Independent school sixth forms — Hampton, Lady Eleanor Holles etc. for families pivoting to private sector at 16.
  • BRIT School (Croydon) — performing arts; clear pipeline given Waldegrave's drama/music culture.
  • Ballet / specialist schools — Royal Ballet Upper School, Elmhurst etc.

Waldegrave's own sixth form recruits ~30% of places externally, partly offsetting the cream lost to Tiffin/Esher.

Kingston Academy — why A-level results look modest

56% A*–B is above the national average of 47% but unremarkable-looking. The standout metric is Progress 8 — top 0.5% nationally (rank 32nd of ~6,500) — meaning pupils make dramatically above-average progress from their starting points. This is exceptional for a non-selective comprehensive serving a mixed-deprivation area. The sixth form is still relatively young and building its reputation.

Grey Court — most transparent on destinations

Publishes full "Destinations 2025 — by university" and "by course" pages. Good Schools Guide confirms ~50% RG and ~70% "top third" with named destinations including Durham, Bristol, Exeter, Edinburgh, Imperial, UCL. Consistent Oxbridge places every cohort since sixth form opened (~2014). Medicine a particular strength.
